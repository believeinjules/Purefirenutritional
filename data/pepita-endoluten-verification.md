# Endoluten: dosing verification (2026-10-13)

**Bottom line:** the sources disagree. Julia's "one capsule every third day" IS printed in a manufacturer-side source: the Russian page on peptid.ru, the official public-reception store of the St. Petersburg Institute of Bioregulation and Gerontology. The English manufacturer catalog (peptideproduct.com, Peptides / NPCRIZ Europe) prints a daily regimen instead. Pepita's generic "1–2 capsules, 1–2 times daily" matches neither source exactly. No document checked names a "maintenance" or "intensive" protocol. Not resolved: the printed box insert for the US-sold lot was not available. Check the physical insert before choosing.

Translation: "Adults over 14: take 1 capsule once every 3 days, in the morning or at midday. Average course length is 20 days to 2 months. If the body is weakened or the person has co-existing conditions, the course may be extended and combined with peptide bioregulators for other organs."

> **Эндолутен Лингвал**: Взрослым от 14 лет принимать под язык за 10–15 минут до еды. Разовая дозировка: 5–6 капель (0,25–0,35 мл). Количество приемов: 3–4 раза в день. Средняя продолжительность курса — 1 месяц. Рекомендуется проводить повторный прием через 3–6 месяцев.

(Lingual only, which Pure Fire does not sell: 5–6 drops under the tongue, 3–4 times a day, 10–15 minutes before meals, 1 month, repeat after 3–6 months.)

> Противопоказания: Индивидуальная непереносимость компонентов, беременность, кормление грудью. Перед применением рекомендуется проконсультироваться с врачом.

(Contraindications: individual intolerance, pregnancy, breastfeeding; consult a doctor before use.)

Control check: peptid.ru capsule directions for 12 other Cytomax/Cytogen products (Bonomarlot, Chelohart, Gotratix, Pielotax, Testoluten, Thyreogen, Ventfort, Visoluten, Vladonix, Cartalax, Crystagen, Vesugen, plus Cerluten, Stamakort, Chitomur, Sigumir, Zhenoluten, Svetinorm and Suprefort) all read "по 1–2 капсулы 1–2 раза в сутки/день во время еды". **Endoluten is the only one with "1 раз в 3 дня."** So on this site it is a product-specific schedule, not a typo carried across the catalog. It still can't be ruled out as a site-specific edit.

## Pack arithmetic (my own arithmetic, not a manufacturer statement)
- At the RU schedule (1 capsule every 3 days): 20 caps ≈ 60 days, 60 caps ≈ 180 days. A 20-capsule pack covers the whole RU course of 20 days to 2 months.
- At the EN schedule (2–4 caps/day): 20 caps = 5–10 days, 60 caps = 15–30 days.
- Pure Fire's "20 caps = one 10-day cycle" only fits the EN schedule at 2 caps/day.

## Recommendation for Pepita (proposal, not applied)
Don't serve the generic template for Endoluten. Until Julia confirms against the physical insert, say that the manufacturer documents differ: the Russian institute store says 1 capsule every 3 days (morning or midday, 20 days–2 months), the European catalog says 1–2 capsules twice daily with meals for 1 month, and the customer should follow their package insert or clinician. Also remove or review "Maintenance/Intensive", "One 10-day cycle", "clinically proven to lengthen telomeres" and "30–40% lifespan" from the product page (all unsourced).
